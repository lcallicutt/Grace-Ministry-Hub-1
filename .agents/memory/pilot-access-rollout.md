---
name: Pilot access rollout
description: The agreed sequencing for initial access, authentication, and payments.
---

Run Grace Ministry Hub first as a manually controlled free pilot lasting 30 days. Do not assume self-service accounts, church-level usage controls, or payment collection are part of the initial pilot. Add login and payment infrastructure after the pilot period.

**Why:** The creator wants to personally control usage during the free trial before introducing account and billing complexity.

**How to apply:** Keep near-term pilot work compatible with manual access management. Treat authentication, account ownership, entitlements, and payments as a later rollout phase unless the creator explicitly changes the sequence.
